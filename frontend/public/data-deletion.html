Meta / Facebook Platform Compliance

User Data Deletion Instructions

At Readlr - The Reading Chronicles, we respect your privacy and are committed to protecting your personal data. In accordance with the Meta (Facebook) Platform terms, this page outlines how you can request the deletion of all data associated with your Facebook login on Readlr.

Option 1: In-App Account Deletion (Instant)

You can permanently delete your Readlr account and all associated data directly within the application:

  1. Log in to your Readlr account.
  2. Open Account Settings from your learner profile or navigation menu.
  3. Under Danger Zone, click Delete Account.
  4. Confirm deletion by typing DELETE and entering your guardian password if prompted.
  5. All learner progress, audio checkpoint records, collected stickers, and login credentials will be permanently erased immediately.

Option 2: Delete Data via Facebook App Settings

If you signed in using Facebook and want to remove Readlr's access to your Facebook account and request data deletion:

  1. Log in to your Facebook account.
  2. Navigate to Settings & Privacy > Settings.
  3. In the left sidebar, click Apps and Websites.
  4. Locate Readlr in the list of active apps and click Remove.
  5. Check the option to "Delete posts, videos or events that Readlr posted on your timeline" (if applicable) and confirm by clicking Remove.

Option 3: Manual Deletion Request

If you are unable to access your account or wish to verify complete data erasure, please email our support team at:
Email: support@readlr.com (or contact the application administrator)
Subject: Facebook User Data Deletion Request

Please include your full name and the email address associated with your Facebook account. Our team will process your request and permanently purge your data from our database within 48 hours.
