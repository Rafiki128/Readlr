Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 2026

Readlr - The Reading Chronicles ("Readlr", "we", "our") is committed to protecting the privacy of our learners, educators, and guardians. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and safeguard personal information when you use our educational web platform.

1. Information We Collect

  • Account Information: When you register or log in using email, Google, or Facebook, we collect your name, email address, and role.
  • Learning Progress & Evidence: Completed books, guided lessons, sound explorations, stickers earned, and audio checkpoint outcomes.
  • Audio Data: Spoken audio recorded during independent reading checkpoints is processed transiently for speech analysis and accuracy assessment.

2. How We Use Information

  • To deliver personalized phonics and reading practice.
  • To maintain persistent curriculum state across learning sessions.
  • To authenticate users and ensure guardian-verified access.

3. Data Deletion & Rights

You have the right to access, update, or permanently delete your account and personal data at any time. For complete instructions, please refer to our User Data Deletion Instructions.

4. Contact Us

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy, please contact our team at support@readlr.com.
